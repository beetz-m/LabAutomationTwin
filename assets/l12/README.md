# L12: automated liquid-handling station

L12 implements Cell 01's `sdt/components/L12_LiquidHandler.yaml` and binds to `/World/Cell01/Stations/L12_LiquidHandler`,
with the frames `deck_frame`, `tip_rack_frames[]`, `reagent_rack_frames[]`, `plate_nest` and `pipette_tip_frame`.

**Design basis.** Cell 01's baseline is a "Hamilton ZEUS LT OEM module integrated in custom Cell01 station". This is
a project-owned single-channel gantry liquid handler in that spirit, not a Hamilton model.

Everything in this folder except `README.md` is generated by `build_l12.py`.

## Build and test

```bash
env_cad/bin/python assets/l12/build_l12.py              # deck + gantry + channel (l12.usda) and the tip (tip.usda)
env_isaacsim/bin/python scripts/test_l12_physics.py     # stand-alone station, 10 checks
env_isaacsim/bin/python scripts/test_l12_robot.py       # R02 places the plate, L12 doses, R02 takes it back (6)
```

## Structure

| Part | What |
|---|---|
| **Deck** | 0.92 × 0.46 m on the central bench at (0.15, 0.25), its Cell 01 location. It holds the plate nest (the shared static SBS nest, seat 52 mm above the worktop = 0.972 m, toward R02's rail), an SBS 96 tip rack, a reagent block with 4 tube positions and a barcode reader each, a tip waste bin, and a plate barcode reader at the nest. |
| **Gantry** | `GantryXSlide` (back rail, −430…+400 mm), `GantryYSlide` (cantilevered arm, −150…+160 mm) and `ChannelZSlide` (one ZEUS-class channel, −150…0 mm). It parks at the west end, clear of R02's approach to the nest. |
| **Tips** | `tip.usda` is a 50 µL-class conductive tip as a free rigid body, with a collar collider only (the cone hangs in the rack hole). Tips are consumables loaded at runtime with identities (`labware:tipId`). The channel picks them with a surface gripper on a collider-free TipMount body (`author_l12`, the R02 coupler pattern) and drops them into the waste bin. |
| **Reagents** | 2 mL tubes added at runtime (`add_reagent_tube`) with `labware:barcode`, `reagent:id`, `reagent:lot` and a physical `reagent:volume_uL`. The controller's inventory is a separate belief that can be wrong. |

## Liquid model and sensors (`twin/l12_liquid.py`)

**Liquid:** tracked volumes and contents per tube and well; the surface height comes from volume and radius. There
is no fluid simulation, as Cell 01's "hybrid" representation allows. The coupling to physics is the tip: liquid-level
detection, aspiration and dispense use the real pose of the mounted tip rigid body.

**Sensor models** (assumptions, not ZEUS data; no biological parameters, D016):
- **cLLD:** the conductive tip touches the surface (0.1 mm).
- **Pressure while flowing:** pressure = −6 mbar per µL/s while aspirating liquid and −0.3 per µL/s while
  aspirating air. A near-occlusion (clog) multiplies the resistance by 20, so the −120 mbar channel limit cuts the flow.
- **Pressure while dispensing:** the mirror image of aspiration.
- **Noise:** 0.4 mbar.
- **Judging a trace:** the plateau must be within 30 % of expected, steady for at least 80 % of the flow, and must
  never hit the clog limit.

## Controller (`twin/l12_controller.py`): `dose_well`

`dose_well` runs as one contract sequence:

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1. Plate | Plate barcode read at the nest, plus the seat sensors. |
| 2. Reagent | Reagent barcode, id and lot read at its position; a mismatch aborts before any tip is picked. |
| 3. Tip | `pick_tip`: gripper attach, then the tip-presence ray must read the expected tip id; otherwise `tip_missing`. |
| 4. Liquid level | `detect_liquid_surface`: slow descent until cLLD fires. The surface height must agree with the inventory within 1.5 mm; otherwise abort before aspiration. |
| 5. Aspirate | 2 mm submerged, with liquid following and a pressure trace. |
| 6. Dispense | At the requested height in the well. The real tip end must be within 1 mm of the bound well centre. |
| 7. Completion | Pressure back to baseline; the volume is inferred from the pressure trace, never the commanded value. |
| 8. Eject | Tip ejected into the waste; the presence ray must see it leave. |

Every dose yields the eight monitors of Cell 01's `dose` designator. `twin/episode_records.py: l12_dose_record`
builds the record with Cell 01's own `build_l12_dose_designator`, `validate_l12_dose` and `build_l12_neem_record`. A
valid dose goes to `culture_sdt_commit`; anything else goes to `H4_or_human_escalation` (an ambiguous dose is not
committed).

## Verified (2026-10-16, `scripts/test_l12_physics.py`)

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Nominal dose: 10 µL into A03 | All 8 monitors pass and the dose commits. Surface found 1.1 mm from the inventory's expectation. Pressure plateau −12.0 / +12.0 mbar. Inferred 9.997 µL. Tube 1000 → 990 µL, well 100 → 110 µL. Tip end 0.001 mm from the well centre. Tip in the waste bin. |
| Wrong reagent in the position | Aborted before tip and aspiration. |
| Tip taken from the next rack position | No tip seen; aborted, nothing aspirated. |
| Reagent tube holding 60 µL (inventory: 1000 µL) | cLLD finds the surface 13.6 mm low; aborted before aspiration. |
| Plate removed | Identity and seating fail; aborted. |
| Records | All validate against the Cell 01 schemas; only the nominal dose commits. |

**`scripts/test_l12_robot.py`, in the Cell 01 overlay: 7/7.**
- R02 placed the plate on the nest (0.08 mm off).
- While R02 was still over the nest, L12 refused to dose (`deck_airspace_occupied`).
- With R02 parked, the dose of 10 µL into B05 passed all 8 monitors and committed: 9.997 µL inferred, tip end 0.03 mm
  from the well centre.
- R02 returned the plate to the handover nest (0.2 mm off).

Two findings came from that test:
- **Airspace interlock:** R02 had been left hovering over the nest, and the gantry drove into it. L12 now has a deck
  airspace monitor: horizontal beams above the gantry's working height that ignore L12's own parts. It refuses or
  pauses motion while anything else is in the airspace.
- **Height bug:** the liquid-level descent fed world heights to a station-local target. That only worked with the
  station on the floor.

## Limits

- **Liquid:** no fluid dynamics, mixing physics or droplet visuals. The pressure and level sensors are models.
- **Tips:** the tip cone has no collider. The channel never moves at low Z except over its target, so a tip crash
  into a well wall is not simulated.
- **Volume reference:** L21's optional balance / volume reference is not built, so L12's volumetric accuracy is not
  calibrated.
