# R03 / H2: clean sample AMR with deterministic dock

R03 implements Cell 01's `sdt/components/R03_AMR_H2_Dock.yaml`:
- **AMR:** bound to `/World/Cell01/Robots/R03_SampleAMR`, with the frames `base_link`, `carrier_cabinet`,
  `dock_approach`, `dock_frame` and `handover_carrier_frame`.
- **Dock:** `/World/Cell01/Stations/H2_AMR_Dock`, the contract's `dock_usd_path`. That prim does not exist in the
  Cell 01 USD; the overlay defines it.

**Design basis.** Cell 01's baseline is a "MiR100-class simulation baseline with Cell01 sealed sample cabinet". These
are project-owned proxies, not MiR models.

Everything in this folder except `README.md` is generated by `build_r03.py`.

## Placement (layout P1 amendment)

The AMR docks heading west along y = −0.28 m. Its carrier nest sits 60 mm off-centre toward R01, so the carrier
seat is at the AMR dock site (−1.45, −0.22, 0.96).

At first the nest was centred, with the seat at (−1.40, −0.28). The layout's reachability check passed there, but the
first full-cell run found that R01's planner could not approach it: the best configuration had σ_min 0.089 (it needs
0.10) and 0° joint margin. At the new site the best approaches have σ_min 0.135 and 27–65° of margin.

Clearances:
- The low chassis (0.35 m) passes under the bench edge.
- The cabinet stays 15 mm south of the bench.
- The low dock (under 0.55 m) stays outside R01's pedestal.

The site moved from y −0.12 because a north-facing dock would have hit R01's pedestal and the bench. The layout
check now treats low parts (`low_zones`) separately: they cannot conflict with R01's arm (≥ 0.72 m) or with the
stations' elevated door swings and trays.

The R03 prim's frame is the AMR's map frame, equal to the docked pose (`dock_frame` = origin, +X = docking
direction). `dock_approach` is 1.0 m before it.

## Structure

| Part | What |
|---|---|
| **Planar base** | Fixed anchor → `PlanarXJoint` → `PlanarYJoint` → `YawJoint` → BaseLink. The controller drives it with differential-drive (unicycle) kinematics. Wheels are visual. |
| **BaseLink** | 890 × 580 × 352 mm chassis, a bumper with a central V-nose dock target and charging contacts, and a sealed cabinet with the carrier nest on top. The nest is the shared static SBS nest, seat 960 mm, 60 mm right of the centreline, with 4 carrier presence sensors and AprilTag #31. |
| **Lid** | `LidSlide`, 340 mm back: the cabinet hood over the carrier. Closed in transit; opens only when docked (dock interlock). |
| **H2** | Two V-guide wedges (±45 mm capture, 3 mm clearance when seated), two stop blocks with charging contacts, a notch for the V-nose, a tower with lamps. Four sensor rays look at the AMR: two on the bumper face (depth and yaw) and two on the V-nose faces (lateral = (d_R − d_L) / 2). |

## Controller (`twin/r03_controller.py`)

**`navigate(route, reverse=False)`**
- Pure pursuit on the estimated pose (encoders plus localisation; a bias can be injected).
- Limits: v ≤ 0.4 m/s, |ω| ≤ 0.8 rad/s; it turns in place above 60° of heading error.
- Reverse driving backs out of the dock zone, because turning in place there would swing the cabinet into the bench.
- It stops on a cabinet opening in transit or on lost localisation.

**`dock(compliant=True)`**
- Starts from `dock_approach`. The lateral and yaw drives go compliant, so the V-guides centre the AMR
  mechanically, and it moves forward at 0.05 m/s to the stop.
- It is accepted only when the dock sensors read depth within 1.5 mm, lateral within 2 mm and yaw within 0.3°.
  The drives are then latched stiff at the reached pose. The contract's `must_not_assume`: arrival near H2 is not
  the same as docked.
- Otherwise `dock_misalignment`: the AMR backs out to the approach pose, then relocalises and retries.

**`open_cabinet` / `close_cabinet`, `undock`, `relocalize`, `load_carrier`.** `load_carrier` refuses non-clean
carriers (clean-sample AMR).

**Logging.** Pose, cabinet state, carrier presence, battery and localisation health are logged at 2 Hz as the
transport evidence. A transport becomes a Cell 01 `transport` record.

## Verified (2026-10-16, `scripts/test_r03.py`, 9/9)

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Inbound | From outside the cell to `dock_approach` within 2 mm in 15 s; cabinet closed and carrier present throughout. Opening the cabinet undocked was refused. |
| Dock | Depth −1.0 mm, lateral −0.001 mm, yaw 0.0°. |
| Cabinet open | Carrier seated 0.35 mm from `handover_carrier_frame`. |
| Undock | Back at the approach pose; H2 free. |
| 30 mm localisation bias, stiff drives | Stalled on the guide; the sensors read 6.4 mm short, 4.0 mm lateral and 0.84° yaw. Backed out, relocalised; the compliant retry docked at 0.0 mm and 0.0°. |
| Lid opened in transit | Stopped (`cabinet_open_in_transit`), closed, reset. |
| Lost localisation | Stopped, relocalised, left the cell. |
| Record | The inbound transport validates as a Cell 01 `transport` record and commits. |

## Limits

- **Base model:** a kinematic planar base with PD drives, not wheel–floor contact. No obstacle sensing and no site
  map.
- **Faults and power:** localisation faults are injected states. Battery drain and charging are bookkeeping only.
- **Carrier:** the carrier is the SBS plate itself in the cabinet nest. A sealed multi-plate carrier and the lower
  shelves are visual only.
