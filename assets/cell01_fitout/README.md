# Cell 01 fit-out: enclosure, machine bench, supports, L12 hood

This folder holds the architectural parts of Cell 01, as specified in the Complete Design and Implementation Specification
v0.3:
- §4.1 safety enclosure;
- §4.2 central automation bench;
- §18 photorealistic asset rules.

They are fitted to layout P1. All are **project-owned proxies** of the classes in the CAD acquisition register:
- an item Line 8 frame;
- Labconco-class cabinets;
- Harmony XB4- and PITestop-class controls.

No manufacturer CAD is used. Everything except this README is generated by `build_fitout.py`. The shared materials come
from `assets/materials/`, and the geometry from `assets/common/meshkit.py`.

```bash
env_cad/bin/python assets/cell01_fitout/build_fitout.py     # after tools/layout_p1.py
env_cad/bin/python tools/compose_cell01_overlay.py          # places them in scenes/cell01_twin.usda
```

### Enclosure E01

The frame is 45 mm aluminium profiles on the 6.0 × 4.5 m interior envelope, 2.5 m high, with floor anchors. The
robot-facing sides have 8 mm laminated glass. The rear service side has sheet panels and the AICOR Cell 01 panel.

Doors and openings:
- **D1:** the interlocked human-access door, at the front, x −1.12…−0.08.
- **D2 and D3:** rear service doors.
- **AMR portal:** x 0.58…1.72, where R03 enters, with a light curtain and muting sensors.

Controls and signals:
- E-stops front-left, front-right (on the HMI pedestal) and rear.
- The HMI on a swivel arm.
- Stack lights on three corners.
- Two overview-camera housings.
- Cable trunking.
- Signs.

### L12 hood

The front and the two sides are glazed. A partial roof carries an LED bar. There is a lift-up front door with an
interlock.

The **robot half of the deck stays open**, guarded by light-curtain columns: from local y −0.03, i.e. world y ≥ 0.22.
This is a measured requirement, not a style choice. R02's carry posture passes over L12 at y ≈ 0.37 m and 1.2–1.5 m
height. A full hood would have put its side panel 50 mm into R02's swept volume (see
[Verification](#verification)).

## Verification

`tools/fitout_clearance.py` replays R02's recorded joint trajectories (`R02_TRACE=<file>`, 20 Hz) through the
URDF kinematics. It models the arm as conservative capsules, plus the tool and a held plate, and measures the
clearance to every fit-out collider.

The results below are from the episode run that recorded the trace:

| Fit-out part | Min clearance |
|---|---|
| Full-depth L12 hood (first design) | **−50 mm** (rejected) |
| L12 hood as built (robot half open) | see the top-level README (final run) |

The regressions and all five episodes were re-run on the fitted overlay. Their contact monitors report robot contacts
with every collider, so a contact with a fit-out part would show there; the results are in the top-level README.

## Limits

- **Doors:** the enclosure and storage doors are articulated, but nothing drives them in the Cell 01 episodes; they
  stay closed. `twin/site_doors.py` opens and closes them and turns their state into the interlock; it is tested in
  `scripts/test_site_route.py`.
- **Safety model:** the light curtain and the interlock are twin-level models with no safety rating.
- **Supports:** the cabinet doors are not articulated (they are static fronts).
