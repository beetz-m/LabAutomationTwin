# L09 Automated Plate Hotel: articulated twin asset (v1)

L09 is a project-owned, LPE220-class design. The Cell 01 baseline is the LiCONiC LiCotel LPE220 family, but its CAD has to be requested, so this is a dimensioned design, not a product model. No product specification is claimed, and the slot count is a parameter.

It implements:
- `sdt/components/L09_PlateHotel.yaml`;
- the Cell 01 binding: `load_frame`, `transfer_nest`, `slot_frames[]` and `service_door_frame`.

Everything in this folder except `README.md` is generated by `build_l09.py`. The shared code lives in
`assets/common/`: `twin_cad.py` holds the CAD/USD helpers, and `sbs_shuttle.py` is the SBS shuttle nest that H4 also uses.

## Build, check, test, render

```bash
cd ~/Documents/LabAutomationTwin
env_cad/bin/python assets/l09/build_l09.py              # CAD -> STEP + USD layers (~10 s)
env_cad/bin/python tools/check_store_clearance.py --device l09  # swept interference check of every handler sequence (CPU)

env_isaacsim/bin/python scripts/render_l09.py [--quick] # path-traced stills, posed by the controller
```

## Design

- **Robot interface:** the same shuttle-presented SBS nest as H4, so the FR3 hand-off validated on H4 applies unchanged.
  - The seat height above the base is the same (72 mm).
  - The nest is presented 200 mm in front of the housing, through a motorised gate that slides up.
  - `transfer_nest` is the robot target.
- **Cassette:** 14 addressable slots at 30 mm pitch. Plates rest on two ledges under their short ends, in an open frame (front and back posts per side).
- **Internal handler:** a lift (Z) carrying a fork (Y) with a 2 mm tine.
  - The tine slides under a plate between the ledges, or through the nest's full-depth channel.
  - A post at the tine's front end drops through a 12 mm slot in the tray to an arm below the tray. So the arm never crosses the nest, the tray or the cassette.
  - The fork stroke (140 mm) exceeds the tray depth plus the post width. The post is therefore in front of the tray at q = 0 and behind it at full stroke, which makes descending at either end clear.
- **Sensing:**
  - **Identity:** a barcode reader at the stowed nest reads the plate's −X short end. Identity is what the ray actually hits (`labware:barcode` on the plate prim), never the slot address.
  - **Slots:** each slot has an occupancy ray.
  - **Nest:** 4 seat sensors.
  - **Doors:** gate, door and shuttle state come from the joints.
- **Service door:** on the +X side, with a window and an interlock. While it is open the handler holds position; it resumes on closing.

## Structure

| Link | Joint | Range | Drive |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Body` | `RootJoint` (fixed) | — | — |
| `Gate` | `GateSlide` (Z) | 0–90 mm | 50 kN/m servo |
| `Shuttle` | `ShuttleSlide` (Y, +q presents) | 0–200 mm | 2 kN/m |
| `Lift` | `LiftSlide` (Z), q = 0 is the tine 1.5 mm below the seat | −1 to 474 mm | 200 kN/m servo |
| `Lift/Fork` | `ForkSlide` (Y), 0 = channel, 140 = slot column | 0–140 mm | 50 kN/m servo |
| `ServiceDoor` | `ServiceDoorHinge` (Z) | 0–100° | weak closer |

The servo stiffness keeps gravity sag below 0.1 mm. Softer drives sagged 0.7–1.3 mm and timed out as false jams.

The articulation never sleeps (the same stale-wake issue found on H4). Collision uses 58 boxes plus 9 convex colliders.

## Runtime

`twin/l09_controller.py` (`L09Controller`) provides these commands:
- `present_for_load`
- `store(plate_id, slot)`
- `retrieve(plate_id)`
- `stow`
- `reserve(slot, plate_id)`
- `reset_fault(by)`: re-homes the handler, and refuses if the tine may be under a nest plate

Each internal sequence is a generator advanced once per physics step, with ramped axis trajectories.

Faults follow the contract:

| Fault | Response |
|---|---|
| `slot_identity_mismatch` | stop, keep the pre-fault inventory, recommend quarantine |
| `double_occupancy_or_jam` | stop |
| `transfer_nest_not_clear` | block robot placement |

## Verified (2026-10-15)

**`test_l09_physics.py`: 11 of 11 pass.**
- **Store:** plates stored into slots 3, 13 (the top slot) and 0 sit within 1.2 mm of their slot frames.
- **Identity:** a mismatch is caught at transfer, the inventory is unchanged, and the plate is stored correctly after reset.
- **Refusals:** a double occupancy and retrieving onto a nest that isn't clear are both refused.
- **Retrieve:** a retrieved plate lands within 0.25 mm of the presented nest, seated and identity-verified.
- **Interlock:** the service door halts the handler and it resumes on closing.

## Known limits

- **Hardware:** not a LiCONiC product model. A single cassette, no climate control (`state:climate_state` = "ambient (not modelled)"), and no barcode decoding: identity is the plate prim's attribute.
- **Robot hand-off:** not yet re-run against L09 specifically. The nest interface and seat height are identical to H4's, where 16 of 16 FR3 trials pass.
- **Placement:** in Cell 01, layout proposal P1 puts L09's transfer nest at (−1.30, 0.85), 0.56 m from R01 and 0.46 m from R02. That's pending your decision, because P1 also moves R01 and H4.
