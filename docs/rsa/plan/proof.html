Motion Plan Refinement Proof

Does the motion-level plan that Giskard executes carry out the culture-level plan, and satisfy every constraint stated on the way? It does, relative to six stated assumptions about the controller, the sensors and the devices. Seven proof obligations link the four levels. Each one is discharged by an SMT proof or a mechanical check that this page reports.

Result
Under A1–A6, every execution of the 111-motion plan either
  1. realizes the culture plan: every physical and knowledge effect, in the derived order, at 0, 48 and 72 h of culture time, with the control culture untreated, while interlocks, L12 airspace, identity, environment before store, calibration before imaging, the 600 s window, contamination freedom, tool hygiene, plate level and collision distance all hold; or
  2. is stopped by a monitor and ends in a failure handler whose guarantee holds (the goal, a safe state, or containment).

Proved: 7 of 7 obligations hold; 8 of 8 negative controls are caught. Produced by rsa/refinement_proof.py, which writes rsa/out/refinement_proof.json.

3 · Assumptions

What the proof takes as given

A motion statechart is a soft optimization problem. Its level-keeping and collision terms are weighted slacks, and convergence and contact are not modelled. The proof therefore makes every soft part hold-or-cancel: a monitor ends the motion when the property is violated. Whatever the proof cannot check itself is listed here, together with how it is covered.

5 · Limits of the result

What is not proved

  • Progress. A1 says that a motion ends or is cancelled. It does not say that the motion reaches its goal. The proof gives correctness or a guaranteed safe failure, not that the experiment completes.
  • Partly detected faults. Branch (ii) covers only faults that a monitor detects. 6 of the 27 fault instances in the process models are detected only partly:
    • L12_LiquidHandler: well misregistration, detected by nest sensor + vision
    • L17_BrightFieldImager: plate misseated, detected by nest sensor + pose
    An undetected misregistration or misseating falls outside the theorem. This is the main gap in trustworthiness.
  • Physics. Compliant insertion into the nests has no Giskard counterpart yet. The 2 mm capture of A3 and the 2° tilt bound are ILLUSTRATIVE.
  • Execution. The Giskard module was generated and its imports were verified, but it has not run: there is no Cell 01 semantic digital twin World yet. R02 collisions are not checked offline.
  • Biology. The culture-level goal is an expected information gain under the surrogate models M1–M3. The proof establishes that the plan does what the design says to the cultures. It makes no claim about what the cultures do.
  • Level of formality. O2, O4 and O5 are SMT proofs. O1, O3, O6 and O7 are exhaustive mechanical checks over finite plans; O6 compares tolerances against A3, it does not prove geometry. The checks of the generated module are syntactic.
