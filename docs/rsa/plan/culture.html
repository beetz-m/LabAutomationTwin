  3 · Mapping to the laboratory
  
Cultures into wells, culture actions into lab actions and instruments

  
Each culture is assigned a vessel. Each physical culture action is refined into one lab action designator (challenge_rescue.ldl), whose instrument role is resolved to a device by its plate slot and its process. Interpreting and analysing need no device: they are computations of the culture SDT. They do need a knowledge precondition the mapping must establish: the image's scale must be known. In Cell 01 that comes from the imager's calibration record, or from a QC run on L21.

  

  

  






  4 · Feasibility of transport and machine tending
  
Can Cell 01 move the plate and tend the machines for each trip?

  
Each trip takes the plate out of the incubator, to the instrument, and back, and tends three machines: the incubator releases and stores the plate, and the liquid handler or the imager receives it in its slot and runs its process. Every check names the evidence that supports it. The ladder runs from what the knowledge says, through a plan, a proof and a projection, to a simulated run and a physics run on the Isaac twin.

  6 · Independence of the culture plan
  
    The same culture plan in thirteen labs
    
  
    The culture plan says what is done to the cultures and what becomes known. It does not say which robots move the plate, which devices dose and image, how the lab is laid out, which safety limits apply, how cleanliness is kept, or how temperature is controlled. Two kinds of evidence, computed by rsa/independence.py:
    vocabulary: no term of any realization vocabulary occurs in the plan's text, and the culture-level derivation reads nothing but that text;
    invariance: the same plan is run in labs that differ in one of those dimensions each, and the plan, its derived order and the lab plan stay the same while the realization changes, sometimes to a refusal.
    

  
    6.1 Vocabulary: nothing of the lab occurs in the plan
    
  
    
    
    The culture plan's terms
    All 107 symbols of culture_plan.ldl (hash a5e27614c98a): cultures, substances, doses, culture times, observations, estimates, the belief over the hypotheses.
    
    
    
      
    The vocabularies are collected from the knowledge itself: planner facts, Cell 01 component records, the constraint and norm registries, plus generic robot and lab terms. Found and fixed while building this check: the plan's interpretation step required "a calibration of the pixel size", and calibration is also the function of the QC device L21. The plan now requires what interpretation actually needs, (a scale (of (an image)) (known true)). How the lab comes to know the scale is the mapping's business.
    
  
    

  
    6.2 Invariance: what changes and what does not
    
  
    Each lab is a variant of the knowledge that changes one dimension, executed on the kernel's symbolic backend. The columns on the left show the culture plan and its derived order, and the lab plan. They are the same everywhere. The columns on the right show the realization, which is highlighted where it differs from Cell 01.
    
  
    
  
    
    
    The plan never changes; the lab answers differently
    
    
    Limits of this demonstration
    The labs are knowledge variants run on the symbolic backend, not new physical twins. The one-robot lab is checked against the facts only: in layout P1, R02 cannot physically reach L10. Model swaps change component records and measurement bindings, not geometry. Environment profiles are names; their setpoints are the lab's own, and the twin asserts none (D016). Robustness to biological variation is a different question, which section 2 addresses.
